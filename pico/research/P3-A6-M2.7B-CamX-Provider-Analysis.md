The Android 12 AOSP ADB client source routes `adb connect HOST[:PORT]` to a `host:connect` query; its transport implementation returns fixed success/already-connected and failure/authentication response strings. This is upstream ADB behavior, not proof of the installed host client's exact version. See [AOSP Android 12 `commandline.cpp`](https://android.googlesource.com/platform/packages/modules/adb/+/refs/tags/android-12.0.0_r13/client/commandline.cpp) and [AOSP `transport_local.cpp`](https://android.googlesource.com/platform/packages/modules/adb/+/ce5fc60b7940cad6d41713e1b37ad1077b0856cd/transport_local.cpp).
